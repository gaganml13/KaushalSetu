# Kaushal Setu 🌉
### Connecting industry needs with practical skill training

Kaushal Setu is a prototype that helps training institutes align their courses with local industry requirements. It brings together job-demand evidence, course analysis, employer feedback, and training-resource planning to recommend practical curriculum updates.

## Key Features
- **Demand Explorer:** Explore skill requirements by role and district.
- **Course Alignment:** Compare skills taught, practised, and assessed.
- **Employer Review:** Review proposed updates and suggest improvements.
- **Training Planner:** Plan batches, trainer availability, equipment, and costs.
- **Learner Pathway:** Identify skill gaps and relevant learning activities.
- **Evidence Register:** Track supporting sources and review history.

## How It Works
Explore demand → Identify course gaps → Propose updates → Get employer feedback → Plan training → Guide learners.

## Prototype Status
Developed for Smart India Hackathon 2026. Uses illustrative data and simulated roles to demonstrate the workflow. Government integrations, employer partnerships, and placement outcomes are not claimed.
